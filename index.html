Overall Score Categories

The RBD Index maps your answers to one of four maturity bands, each signalling a different strategic priority.

85 – 100

By Design

Your revenue system is repeatable and predictable. Growth is no longer dependent on heroics or top performers. The priority shifts to optimization, expansion into new segments, and staying ahead of the market through continuous improvement.

70 – 84

Scalable

A working revenue engine exists with specific, identifiable leaks. You have the foundation to scale, but targeted fixes - in process consistency, coaching cadence, or CRM discipline - will unlock fast and measurable returns without adding cost.

50 – 69

Built on Effort

The elements of a revenue system exist but are not fully connected. Growth is happening through individual effort rather than a shared process. The biggest lever is integrating your people, process and technology so that success is repeatable and not person-dependent.

Under 50

Revenue at Risk

Growth depends on heroics and a small number of key individuals. The foundation needs to be established before adding headcount or marketing spend. Prioritize the lowest-scoring pillar first - that is the binding constraint on everything else.
